**May's Beautifly** (Japanese: **ハルカのアゲハント** *Haruka's Agehunt*) was the first [wild Pokémon](/wiki/Wild_Pok%C3%A9mon "Wild Pokémon") that [May](/wiki/May_(anime) "May (anime)") [caught](/wiki/Caught_Pok%C3%A9mon "Caught Pokémon") in the [Hoenn](/wiki/Hoenn "Hoenn") [region](/wiki/Region "Region"), and her second overall.

## In animation

### History

#### [*Pokémon the Series: Ruby and Sapphire*](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_Ruby_and_Sapphire "Pokémon the Series: Ruby and Sapphire")

##### Hoenn

While assisting [Janet](/wiki/Janet "Janet") in the [Rustboro City](/wiki/Rustboro_City "Rustboro City") [Pokémon Contest](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Contest "Pokémon Contest") in *[All Things Bright and Beautifly!](/wiki/AG013 "AG013")*, May watched Janet's Beautifly perform and decided that she wanted one for herself. In [the next episode](/wiki/AG014 "AG014"), May caught a Wurmple, her first Pokémon, with the help of [Forrest Franklin](/wiki/Forrest_Franklin "Forrest Franklin") and her [Torchic](/wiki/May%27s_Torchic "May's Torchic"). At the end of the day, May introduced her new friend to the rest of the group. When [Brock](/wiki/Brock_(anime) "Brock (anime)") presented lunch, Wurmple quickly ate everyone's food before falling sound asleep, leaving everyone stunned.

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/f/f0/May_Wurmple_gluttonous_nature.png/220px-May_Wurmple_gluttonous_nature.png)](/wiki/File:May_Wurmple_gluttonous_nature.png)Wurmple gorging on food

In *[Which Wurmple's Which?](/wiki/AG021 "AG021")*, May argued with [Jessie](/wiki/Jessie "Jessie") over which one of them had the better Wurmple. After [Max](/wiki/Max "Max") concluded that the two Wurmple were exactly the same, they began battling, but [Team Rocket](/wiki/Team_Rocket_trio "Team Rocket trio") was sent blasting off by [Pikachu](/wiki/Ash%27s_Pikachu "Ash's Pikachu"). May soon realized that she has Jessie's Wurmple and that Jessie has hers. While with Team Rocket, Wurmple caused trouble for them by eating what little food they had, proving to be far more gluttonous than Jessie's Wurmple. Eventually, Wurmple was returned to May in exchange for Jessie's Wurmple.

In *[A Corphish Out of Water](/wiki/AG024 "AG024")*, May and Jessie once again pitted their two Wurmple against each other in battle, where they simultaneously evolved into [Silcoon](/wiki/Silcoon_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Silcoon (Pokémon)") and [Cascoon](/wiki/Cascoon_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Cascoon (Pokémon)"), respectively.

In *[Seeing is Believing!](/wiki/AG028 "AG028")*, Silcoon once again battled Cascoon in an attempt to prove to Jessie that her Pokémon was not a Silcoon, evolving into [Beautifly](/wiki/Beautifly_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Beautifly (Pokémon)") in the process. Later on, Cascoon evolved as well, and the two fought again, with Beautifly winning.

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/1/13/AG028.png/220px-AG028.png)](/wiki/File:AG028.png)Beautifly vs Dustox

In [*Now* That's *Flower Power!*](/wiki/AG033 "AG033"), Beautifly was seen practicing with May for the [Slateport City](/wiki/Slateport_City "Slateport City") Pokémon Contest as it was having trouble using its [Silver Wind](/wiki/Silver_Wind_(move) "Silver Wind (move)") attack. Its routine had some flaws that would not look good in a Contest, as [Drew](/wiki/Drew "Drew") pointed out.

In *[Win, Lose or Drew!](/wiki/AG035 "AG035")*, May used Beautifly for the Slateport Contest. Beautifly's performance impressed the [judges](/wiki/Contest_Judge "Contest Judge") enough to get May past the [Appeals Round](/wiki/Appeal "Appeal"). Unfortunately, their success was short-lived when Beautifly was defeated by [Drew's Roselia](/wiki/Drew%27s_Roselia "Drew's Roselia") in the first match of the [Battle Round](/wiki/Contest_Battle "Contest Battle").

Later, in *[Come What May!](/wiki/AG051 "AG051")*, Beautifly entered the [Fallarbor Town](/wiki/Fallarbor_Town "Fallarbor Town") Contest, where it impressed the judges in the Appeal Round. In the second round, it went up against Jessie's Dustox. It initially struggled but was able to win by dealing a variety of damage to Dustox, which caused the rainbow device on her bow tie to break, revealing her as a cheater. In the final round, Beautifly battled [Grace](/wiki/Grace_(Hoenn) "Grace (Hoenn)") and her [Medicham](/wiki/Medicham_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Medicham (Pokémon)"). After a hard-fought battle, Beautifly was declared the victor, earning May her first [Ribbon](/wiki/Ribbon "Ribbon").

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/1/1b/May_Beautifly_Bulbasaur_Hoenn_Grand_Festival.png/220px-May_Beautifly_Bulbasaur_Hoenn_Grand_Festival.png)](/wiki/File:May_Beautifly_Bulbasaur_Hoenn_Grand_Festival.png)Beautifly and Bulbasaur in the Hoenn Grand Festival

In *[Cruisin' for a Losin'](/wiki/AG078 "AG078")*, Beautifly got May through to the second round of the [R1](/wiki/Rank_(Contest) "Rank (Contest)") [Rubello](/wiki/Rubello_Town "Rubello Town") Contest with a beautiful [combination](/wiki/Contest_combination "Contest combination") of [Morning Sun](/wiki/Morning_Sun_(move) "Morning Sun (move)") and Silver Wind.

In *[That's Just Swellow](/wiki/AG080 "AG080")*, May used Beautifly in the [Crossgate PokéRinger](/wiki/Crossgate_Pok%C3%A9Ringer "Crossgate PokéRinger"). [James](/wiki/James "James"), using Dustox, took advantage of Beautifly's attack to quickly win their round, riding the attack to score and knocking May out of the competition.

In *[The Scuffle of Legends](/wiki/AG098 "AG098")*, Beautifly and [Mudkip](/wiki/Brock%27s_Mudkip "Brock's Mudkip") distracted [Archie](/wiki/Archie "Archie"), giving [Groudon](/wiki/Kyogre_and_Groudon_(Ruby_and_Sapphire_series)#Groudon "Kyogre and Groudon (Ruby and Sapphire series)") the opportunity to defeat [Kyogre](/wiki/Kyogre_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Kyogre (Pokémon)").

In *[Hi Ho Silver Wind!](/wiki/AG121 "AG121")*, Beautifly managed to get past the preliminary Appeals Round of the [Hoenn Grand Festival](/wiki/Hoenn_Grand_Festival "Hoenn Grand Festival") by using Silver Wind. Later, in *[Rhapsody in Drew](/wiki/AG123 "AG123")* Beautifly teamed up with [Bulbasaur](/wiki/May%27s_Bulbasaur "May's Bulbasaur") in the first battle round against [Harley's Cacturne](/wiki/Harley%27s_Cacturne "Harley's Cacturne") and [Banette](/wiki/Banette_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Banette (Pokémon)"). The duo showed off stunning combinations, such as with Silver Wind and [Razor Leaf](/wiki/Razor_Leaf_(move) "Razor Leaf (move)"), and won the match.

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/7/7b/May_and_Beautifly.png/220px-May_and_Beautifly.png)](/wiki/File:May_and_Beautifly.png)Beautifly and May

Before heading off to the [Kanto](/wiki/Kanto "Kanto") [region](/wiki/Region "Region"), May stopped by Petalburg City and left her Beautifly under the care of her mother, [Caroline](/wiki/Caroline "Caroline").

##### Kanto

May returned Beautifly to her party in *[The Unbeatable Lightness of Seeing!](/wiki/AG187 "AG187")*. It, alongside [Skitty](/wiki/May%27s_Skitty "May's Skitty"), created a new Contest combination by combining [Blizzard](/wiki/Blizzard_(move) "Blizzard (move)") and Psychic to make a snow tornado. It and [Combusken](/wiki/May%27s_Combusken "May's Combusken") later battled Drew's Roselia and [Butterfree](/wiki/Butterfree_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Butterfree (Pokémon)") in a practice Contest battle, where they eventually lost.

#### [*Pokémon the Series: Diamond and Pearl*](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_Diamond_and_Pearl "Pokémon the Series: Diamond and Pearl")

Beautifly reappeared in *[A Full Course Tag Battle!](/wiki/DP076 "DP076")* when May came to [Sinnoh](/wiki/Sinnoh "Sinnoh") to compete in the [Wallace Cup](/wiki/Wallace_Cup "Wallace Cup"). In *[Strategy with a Smile!](/wiki/DP079 "DP079")*, May used Beautifly to battle [Zoey's Glameow](/wiki/Zoey%27s_Glameow "Zoey's Glameow") in the semifinals of the competition. Glameow proved to be a tough opponent, using the water battlefield to drench Beautifly and amplify its [Shock Wave](/wiki/Shock_Wave_(move) "Shock Wave (move)"). The Catty Pokémon proceeded with [Iron Tail](/wiki/Iron_Tail_(move) "Iron Tail (move)"), but Beautifly used Silver Wind towards the pool and another curtain of water was raised, leading Glameow to be caught off guard and surprised with Psychic. Afterward, Beautifly healed itself with a beautiful Morning Sun and used a speedy [Aerial Ace](/wiki/Aerial_Ace_(move) "Aerial Ace (move)"). Glameow responded with a [Shadow Claw](/wiki/Shadow_Claw_(move) "Shadow Claw (move)") and the two Pokémon collided center stage just as the time ran out. Beautifly won the battle on points, advancing May to the finals.

### Personality and characteristics

As a Wurmple, it would gorge itself on food and fall asleep, unlike Jessie's more sedate and well-behaved Wurmple. It also had a remarkably lazy personality. However, when it evolved into Silcoon and then Beautifly, it became more calm and mellow. As a Beautifly, it often likes to sit atop May's head, and it appears to only do so to those it considers her friends, such as when it landed on top of [Ash](/wiki/Ash_Ketchum "Ash Ketchum")'s head after it and May came to [Sinnoh](/wiki/Sinnoh "Sinnoh"). She also frequently used it alongside [Ash's Swellow](/wiki/Ash%27s_Swellow "Ash's Swellow") to look for [Team Rocket](/wiki/Team_Rocket_trio "Team Rocket trio") whenever they got away with something.

## Trivia

- Beautifly is [May](/wiki/May_(anime) "May (anime)")'s only Pokémon whose base stage is not an option for the [hero Pokémon](/wiki/Hero_Pok%C3%A9mon "Hero Pokémon") in [Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time, Explorers of Darkness](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Time_and_Explorers_of_Darkness "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time and Explorers of Darkness"), or [Explorers of Sky](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Explorers_of_Sky "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Sky").
- Beautifly is May's only Pokémon with an even gender ratio.
- After evolving into Beautifly, it stopped eating [Pokémon food](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_food "Pokémon food"), instead switching to sugar cubes.
- Beautifly is the first Pokémon belonging to one of [Ash's friends](/wiki/Ash%27s_friends "Ash's friends") to use a [Bug-type](/wiki/Bug_(type) "Bug (type)") move.
- Beautifly was May's first Pokémon to evolve and her first fully evolved Pokémon.
- Beautifly, as a Wurmple, was the only Pokémon that May [caught](/wiki/Caught_Pok%C3%A9mon "Caught Pokémon") by battling, as her other Pokémon were either given to her, chose to go with her, or were caught by accident.
- The opening theme *[I Wanna Be a Hero](/wiki/I_Wanna_Be_a_Hero "I Wanna Be a Hero")* revealed that May's Wurmple would evolve into Beautifly long before she even caught it.
