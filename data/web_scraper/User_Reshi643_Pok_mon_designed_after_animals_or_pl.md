## Plant Pokémon

**Note:** The Pokémon in bud, flower, leaf, tree, tumbleweed, vine and wood sections are based on plants in general. Pokémon based on specific plants can be found in their individual sections.

Food-based Pokémon are often based on variety of plant-based flavors:

- Normal and shiny Swirlix-line: fruit and chocolate
