**Bulbasaur** ([Japanese](/wiki/List_of_Japanese_Pok%C3%A9mon_names "List of Japanese Pokémon names"): **フシギダネ** *Fushigidane*) is a dual-type [Grass](/wiki/Grass_(type) "Grass (type)")/[Poison](/wiki/Poison_(type) "Poison (type)") [Pokémon](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_(species) "Pokémon (species)") introduced in [Generation I](/wiki/Generation_I "Generation I").

It [evolves](/wiki/Evolution "Evolution") into [Ivysaur](/wiki/Ivysaur_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Ivysaur (Pokémon)") starting at [level](/wiki/Level "Level") 16, which evolves into [Venusaur](/wiki/Venusaur_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Venusaur (Pokémon)") starting at level 32.

Along with [Charmander](/wiki/Charmander_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Charmander (Pokémon)") and [Squirtle](/wiki/Squirtle_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Squirtle (Pokémon)"), Bulbasaur is one of the three [first partner Pokémon of Kanto](/wiki/Kanto_first_partner_Pok%C3%A9mon "Kanto first partner Pokémon") available at the beginning of [Pokémon Red, Green](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Red_and_Green_Versions "Pokémon Red and Green Versions"), [Blue](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Red_and_Blue_Versions "Pokémon Red and Blue Versions"), [FireRed, and LeafGreen](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen_Versions "Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen Versions").

## Biology

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/e/e9/Art_Life_20230116_Bulbasaur.jpg/220px-Art_Life_20230116_Bulbasaur.jpg)](/wiki/File:Art_Life_20230116_Bulbasaur.jpg)Bulbasaur using its vines to climb a tree

Bulbasaur is a small, quadrupedal [amphibian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/amphibian "wp:amphibian") [Pokémon](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_(species) "Pokémon (species)") that has blue-green skin with darker patches. It has red eyes with white pupils, pointed, ear-like structures on top of its head, and a short, blunt snout with a wide mouth. Small, pointed teeth are visible in the upper jaw when the mouth is open. Each of its thick legs ends with three sharp claws. On Bulbasaur's back is a green plant bulb that conceals two slender, tentacle-like vines, which grow from a seed planted there at birth. The bulb also provides it with energy through photosynthesis and from the nutrient-rich seeds contained within.

Bulbasaur is sometimes seen seen napping in bright sunlight and can survive for days without eating. It is found in [grasslands](/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_by_habitat#Grassland_Pok%C3%A9mon "List of Pokémon by habitat") and forests throughout the [Kanto](/wiki/Kanto "Kanto") [region](/wiki/Region "Region"). However, due to its status as a [first partner Pokémon](/wiki/First_partner_Pok%C3%A9mon "First partner Pokémon"), it is hard to come by in the wild and is generally found under the ownership of a Trainer. It has been recently seen living in the [Terarium](/wiki/Terarium "Terarium") of [Blueberry Academy](/wiki/Blueberry_Academy "Blueberry Academy"). First partner Pokémon such as Bulbasaur are raised by [Pokémon Breeders](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Breeder_(Trainer_class) "Pokémon Breeder (Trainer class)") to be distributed to new [Trainers](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Trainer "Pokémon Trainer").[[1]](#cite_note-1) Having been raised by [humans](/wiki/Human "Human") from birth, it is regarded as both a rare and well-behaved Pokémon. It is known to be extremely loyal, even after long-term abandonment.[[2]](#cite_note-2) Bulbasaur has demonstrated a nurturing instinct towards younger, weaker Pokémon, such as using its vines to pick up a crying Pokémon and gently rocking it back and forth through the air while singing a "Bulba-by".[[3]](#cite_note-3) Its bulb will flash blue when it is ready to [evolve](/wiki/Evolution "Evolution"), but if it does not want to, it struggles to resist the transformation. Many Bulbasaur gather every year in a hidden garden in Kanto to evolve into [Ivysaur](/wiki/Ivysaur_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Ivysaur (Pokémon)") in a ceremony led by a [Venusaur](/wiki/Venusaur_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Venusaur (Pokémon)").[[4]](#cite_note-4) Its vines are long and strong enough to allow Bulbasaur to grab the branches of trees and pull itself up to reach [Berries](/wiki/Berry "Berry").[[5]](#cite_note-5) As mentioned in [Pokémon Sleep](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep "Pokémon Sleep"), Bulbasaur sits still and basks in sunny spots during its sleep, possibly trying to absorb nutrients from its seed to grow big.[[6]](#cite_note-6)

## In animation

### Main series

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/1/19/Ash_Bulbasaur.png/250px-Ash_Bulbasaur.png)](/wiki/File:Ash_Bulbasaur.png)Bulbasaur in [*Pokémon Journeys: The Series*](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Journeys:_The_Series "Pokémon Journeys: The Series")

#### Major appearances

##### [Ash's Bulbasaur](/wiki/Ash%27s_Bulbasaur "Ash's Bulbasaur")

[Ash](/wiki/Ash_Ketchum "Ash Ketchum")'s Bulbasaur debuted in *[Bulbasaur and the Hidden Village](/wiki/EP010 "EP010")* as one of the Pokémon in the [Hidden Village](/wiki/Hidden_Village "Hidden Village"), thus also marking the species' physical debut. Though initially wary of Ash, the two eventually warmed up to one another, and Bulbasaur challenged him to a [battle](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_battle "Pokémon battle"). Using [Pikachu](/wiki/Ash%27s_Pikachu "Ash's Pikachu"), Ash won the battle, and Bulbasaur was [caught](/wiki/Caught_Pok%C3%A9mon "Caught Pokémon"). Unlike [Charmander](/wiki/Ash%27s_Charmander "Ash's Charmander") and [Squirtle](/wiki/Ash%27s_Squirtle "Ash's Squirtle"), which were caught in the two episodes following that of Bulbasaur, Bulbasaur remained in Ash's [party](/wiki/Party "Party") for most of his [journey](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_journey "Pokémon journey") through [Johto](/wiki/Johto "Johto") until *[Bulbasaur... the Ambassador!](/wiki/EP225 "EP225")*, when Ash sent him to [Professor Oak](/wiki/Professor_Oak_(anime) "Professor Oak (anime)")'s [lab](/wiki/Professor_Oak%27s_Laboratory "Professor Oak's Laboratory") to work as an ambassador between otherwise argumentative groups of Pokémon.

##### [May's Bulbasaur](/wiki/May%27s_Bulbasaur "May's Bulbasaur")

Much later on, while Ash was traveling in the [Hoenn](/wiki/Hoenn "Hoenn") [region](/wiki/Region "Region"), [May](/wiki/May_(anime) "May (anime)") caught her own Bulbasaur after being separated from the group in *[Grass Hysteria!](/wiki/AG073 "AG073")*. She remained with her throughout the rest of her journey in Hoenn, helping her to win the [Purika](/wiki/Purika_City "Purika City") [Contest](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Contest "Pokémon Contest") and place Top 8 in the [regional](/wiki/Hoenn_Grand_Festival "Hoenn Grand Festival") [Grand Festival](/wiki/Grand_Festival "Grand Festival"). When she traveled to [Kanto](/wiki/Kanto "Kanto") to continue her journey with Ash in *[The Right Place and the Right Mime](/wiki/AG133 "AG133")*, May, too, left her Bulbasaur at [Professor Oak's Laboratory](/wiki/Professor_Oak%27s_Laboratory "Professor Oak's Laboratory") to learn from Ash's while she, Ash, [Max](/wiki/Max "Max"), and [Brock](/wiki/Brock_(anime) "Brock (anime)") traveled throughout the Kanto region. May retrieved her during her travels in [Johto](/wiki/Johto "Johto"), where she evolved all the way into a [Venusaur](/wiki/Venusaur_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Venusaur (Pokémon)").

##### [Shauna's Bulbasaur](/wiki/Shauna%27s_Bulbasaur "Shauna's Bulbasaur")

[Shauna](/wiki/Shauna_(anime) "Shauna (anime)")'s first partner Pokémon is a Bulbasaur she received from [Professor Sycamore](/wiki/Professor_Sycamore_(anime) "Professor Sycamore (anime)"). It first appeared in *[Summer of Discovery!](/wiki/XY039 "XY039")*, and it was revealed to have evolved into [Ivysaur](/wiki/Ivysaur_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Ivysaur (Pokémon)") by *[Tag Team Battle Inspiration!](/wiki/XY090 "XY090")*

##### Other

In *[Bulbasaur's Mysterious Garden](/wiki/EP051 "EP051")*, a group of [wild](/wiki/Wild_Pok%C3%A9mon "Wild Pokémon") Bulbasaur gathered in the [Mysterious Garden](/wiki/Mysterious_Garden "Mysterious Garden") and took part in a festival where they all evolved into Ivysaur en masse. They also took Ash's Bulbasaur to the garden with them, though it refused to evolve.

In *[Mewtwo Strikes Back](/wiki/M01 "M01")*, a Bulbasaur was cloned from Ash's own Bulbasaur. The cloned Bulbasaur participated in the battle between the original Pokémon and the cloned Pokémon. Bulbasaur reappeared in *[Mewtwo Returns](/wiki/Mewtwo_Returns "Mewtwo Returns")*.

In *[The Mystery Menace](/wiki/EP102 "EP102")*, a Bulbasaur was abandoned by the [Mayor of Trovitopolis](/wiki/Mayor_of_Trovitopolis "Mayor of Trovitopolis") during his childhood when it wouldn't evolve into Ivysaur. Since then, it grew to a very large size in the sewers. Aided by [Misty](/wiki/Misty_(anime) "Misty (anime)") and [Tracey](/wiki/Tracey_Sketchit "Tracey Sketchit"), Ash found the abandoned Bulbasaur while searching for his own, which had been taken by the giant Bulbasaur. After defeating the arrogant mayor and his personal SWAT team, Ash and his friends left the Bulbasaur with [Nurse Joy](/wiki/Nurse_Joy "Nurse Joy").

In *[Journey to the Starting Line!](/wiki/HS14 "HS14")*, [Gilbert](/wiki/Gilbert_(HS14) "Gilbert (HS14)") was supposed to start his journey as a [Pokémon Trainer](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Trainer "Pokémon Trainer") with one of the three Kanto [first partner Pokémon](/wiki/First_partner_Pok%C3%A9mon "First partner Pokémon"), but they were scared off by [Ash's Tauros](/wiki/Ash%27s_Tauros "Ash's Tauros"), forcing Gilbert to set out to find them with [Professor Oak](/wiki/Professor_Oak_(anime) "Professor Oak (anime)"), [Tracey](/wiki/Tracey_Sketchit "Tracey Sketchit"), and [Delia](/wiki/Delia_Ketchum "Delia Ketchum"). Bulbasaur was the last one to be found, having been stuck in a tree that was surrounded by a group of [Primeape](/wiki/Primeape_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Primeape (Pokémon)"). In the end, Bulbasaur was chosen by Gilbert as his first partner Pokémon.

In *[A Six Pack Attack!](/wiki/AG071 "AG071")*, a Bulbasaur went with Professor Oak on his trip to [Hoenn](/wiki/Hoenn "Hoenn") along with a [Charmander](/wiki/Charmander_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Charmander (Pokémon)") and [Squirtle](/wiki/Squirtle_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Squirtle (Pokémon)").

A Bulbasaur appeared in *[The Right Place and the Right Mime](/wiki/AG133 "AG133")*, where it was one of the [first partner Pokémon](/wiki/First_partner_Pok%C3%A9mon "First partner Pokémon") seen at [Professor Oak's Laboratory](/wiki/Professor_Oak%27s_Laboratory "Professor Oak's Laboratory").

In *[Ivysaur's Mysterious Tower!](/wiki/JN003 "JN003")*, multiple Bulbasaur and Ivysaur traveled to a [Gym](/wiki/Gym_(GO) "Gym (GO)") that was under construction in [Vermilion City](/wiki/Vermilion_City "Vermilion City"). Ash and [Goh](/wiki/Goh "Goh"), who were investigating the outbreak, followed them to the Gym and realized the horde had gone there so the Bulbasaur could absorb the sunlight necessary to evolve into new Ivysaur. They reappeared in a flashback in *[Getting More Than You Battled For!](/wiki/JN046 "JN046")*.

### Cultural impact

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/b/b8/Niue_Dollar_Pok%C3%A9mon.png/250px-Niue_Dollar_Pok%C3%A9mon.png)](/wiki/File:Niue_Dollar_Pok%C3%A9mon.png)$1 coins from [Niue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niue "wp:Niue")

- While Bulbasaur is not considered to be part of the [inspiration for the naming](/wiki/List_of_references_to_Pok%C3%A9mon_in_science "List of references to Pokémon in science") of *[Bulbasaurus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulbasaurus "wp:Bulbasaurus")*, a genus of [dicynodonts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/dicynodont "wp:dicynodont"), it has been noted that the similarities "may not be entirely coincidental".[[7]](#cite_note-7)


### Concept and development

Bulbasaur was designed by [Atsuko Nishida](/wiki/Atsuko_Nishida "Atsuko Nishida").[[8]](#cite_note-8)

### Origin

Bulbasaur is based on a [frog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/frog "wp:frog").[[9]](#cite_note-9)[[10]](#cite_note-CreatorsOfPikachu-10) In particular, its short legs, stout appearance, and ear-like protrusions on its head make it resemble [horned frogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ceratophrys "wp:Ceratophrys"). According to [Ken Sugimori](/wiki/Ken_Sugimori "Ken Sugimori") and [Atsuko Nishida](/wiki/Atsuko_Nishida "Atsuko Nishida"), the latter designed Bulbasaur working backward from Venusaur, meaning Bulbasaur was the last of its evolutionary line to have been designed and was also directly based on Venusaur.[[10]](#cite_note-CreatorsOfPikachu-10) The bulb on its back resembles a [lily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilium "wp:Lilium") or [onion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/onion "wp:onion").

#### Name origin

Bulbasaur is a combination of *[bulb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/bulb "wp:bulb")*[[11]](#cite_note-11)[[12]](#cite_note-INSIDE-12)[[13]](#cite_note-4gamer-13) (a rounded underground storage organ present in some plants) and *[dinosaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/dinosaur "wp:dinosaur")*[[13]](#cite_note-4gamer-13) or *-saurus*[[12]](#cite_note-INSIDE-12) (common suffix used in the names of extinct reptilian creatures, derived from the Ancient Greek word for [lizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/lizard "wp:lizard")). The name also sounds similar to the word *bulbous*.

Fushigidane may be a combination of 不思議 *fushigi* (strange; mysterious) and 種 *tane* (seed), as well as a pun of the phrase 不思議だね *Fushigi da ne?* ("Isn't it strange?").
