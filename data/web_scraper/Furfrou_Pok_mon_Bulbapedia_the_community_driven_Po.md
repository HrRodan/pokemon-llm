**Furfrou** (Japanese: **トリミアン** *Trimmien*) is a Normal-type Pokémon introduced in Generation VI.

While it is not known to evolve into or from any other Pokémon, Furfrou can have its hair trimmed into nine different styles, changing its appearance from its **Natural Form** into one of the following trims, each named after the style its fur is trimmed and dyed in: **Heart Trim**, **Star Trim**, **Diamond Trim**, **Debutante Trim**, **Matron Trim**, **Dandy Trim**, **La Reine Trim**, **Kabuki Trim**, and **Pharaoh Trim**. Furfrou will revert back to its Natural Form if five days have passed since it was trimmed. Other circumstances may also cause it to revert.

(Specifics may differ in past games. Refer to Game data→Form data for these details.)

## Biology

Furfrou is a quadrupedal, canine Pokémon with thick, white fur that covers its black body apart from its paws and face. The fur is thicker and fluffier around its head and tail. It has round, red eyes with blue eyelids, a pointed snout with a round, light blue nose, and long rectangular ears. When its mouth is open, two pointed teeth can be seen on its upper jaw.

Furfrou used to guard the king in the ancient times of Kalos and is known to be loyal to its Trainer.Pokémon Legends: Z-A, a "Furfrou League" has been established in Lumiose City, complete with its own Elite Four and Champion, where all participants only use Furfrou to battle one another.

Prior to Generation VII, Furfrou was the only known Pokémon that could have Fur Coat as an Ability.

### Forms

Furfrou has ten different forms named after the style its fur is trimmed and dyed in: Natural Form, Heart Trim, Star Trim, Diamond Trim, Debutante Trim, Matron Trim, Dandy Trim, La Reine Trim, Kabuki Trim, and Pharaoh Trim.

It is commonly seen with its fur trimmed and dyed into various styles. However, only those it trusts are allowed to cut its fur. In the past, aristocrats would even compete over who could trim its fur into the most exquisite style. Cutting its fur increases the swiftness of its movements.

### Form data

Furfrou has ten different forms. It is in its Natural Form by default and can be groomed into one of nine different trims: Heart Trim, Star Trim, Diamond Trim, Debutante Trim, Matron Trim, Dandy Trim, La Reine Trim, Kabuki Trim, and Pharaoh Trim.

It will remain with one of these trims for five days after being groomed, after which it will revert back to its Natural Form. Furfrou will also lose its trim when it is deposited in a Box (in Generation VI), withdrawn from a Box (in Generation VII), deposited in Pokémon Bank, or deposited in the Pokémon Day Care or Pokémon Nursery. However, it is possible to deposit trimmed Furfrou in Pokémon HOME by transfering it from Pokémon GO via the GO Transporter.

The location at which Furfrou can be groomed differs between games:

- In Pokémon X and Y, Furfrou can be groomed at Friseur Furfrou on Vernal Avenue in Lumiose City for $500. The player gains access to more trims as they become more stylish: the Heart, Star, and Diamond Trims are available initially, the La Reine, Kabuki, and Pharaoh Trims are made available at 10 style points, and the Debutante, Matron, and Dandy Trims are made available at 50 style points.
- In Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire, Furfrou can be groomed at the Pokémon Fan Club in Slateport City for $500.
- In Pokémon Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun, and Ultra Moon, Furfrou can be groomed for $500 by a Punk Girl at Malie City's salon or, in Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon, a Beauty at Hau'oli City's salon.
- In Pokémon Legends: Z-A, Furfrou can be groomed by Mirte in Jaune Sector 6 for $500 after the player completes the "Becoming a Furfrou Trimmer" side mission. More trims become available as the player completes more side missions for Mirte: the Heart, Star, and Diamond Trims are available initially, the Debutante, Matron, and Dandy Trims are made available after completing "Becoming a Pro Furfrou Trimmer", and the La Reine, Kabuki, and Pharaoh Trims are made available after completing "Becoming a Peerless Furfrou Trimmer".

These locations also allow the player to revert a trimmed Furfrou to its Natural Form.

In Pokémon X and Y only, Furfrou's trims are not registered as distinct forms in the Pokédex.

## In animation

### Main series

Natural Form Furfrou in *Pokémon the Series: Sun & Moon*

#### Major appearances

Multiple Furfrou appeared in *Grooming Furfrou!*, including one that belonged to Jessica. It initially did not approve of her as its Pokémon Stylist and thus she had difficulties with it, but their relationship improved after Team Rocket attempted to steal it. This episode featured Furfrou's Heart, Diamond, La Reine, and Kabuki Trims.

A Matron Trim Furfrou appeared in *Awakening the Sleeping Giant!*, under the ownership of Princess Allie. It was used in a battle against Ash, during which it was shocked by Pikachu's Thunderbolt. Because its fur was ruined in the process, and she was not content with Furfrou battling without looking beautiful, Allie forfeited the match.

A Dandy Trim Furfrou appeared in *Dreaming a Performer's Dream!*, under the ownership of Kayleigh. It competed with its Trainer in a Rookie Class Pokémon Showcase. However, their performance was interrupted by a Pancham that appeared onstage and began imitating Furfrou's moves. After Pancham broke a stage lamp and fell on the stage, it ran off the stage in fear.

### Origin

Furfrou appears to be based on a poodle. Its Natural Form may draw some inspiration from Afghan Hounds.

#### Name origin

Furfrou may be a combination of *fur* and *frou-frou* (fancy, overly elaborate).

Trimmien may be a combination of *trimming*, *chien* (French for dog), and possibly a play on the phrase *très bien* (French for excellent).
