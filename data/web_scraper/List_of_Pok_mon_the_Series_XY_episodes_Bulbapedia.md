# List of *Pokémon the Series: XY* episodes

From Bulbapedia, the community-driven Pokémon encyclopedia.

[Jump to navigation](#column-one)[Jump to search](#searchInput)

This is a list of episodes of *[Pokémon the Series: XY](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_XY "Pokémon the Series: XY")*, which first aired between October 17, 2013 and October 27, 2016 in Japan and between January 18, 2014 and January 21, 2017 in the United States, though the first two episodes aired as a preview in the United States on October 19, 2013. These episodes follow [Ash Ketchum](/wiki/Ash_Ketchum "Ash Ketchum") in his [Pokémon journey](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_journey "Pokémon journey") across the [Kalos](/wiki/Kalos "Kalos") [region](/wiki/Region "Region") along with [his new friends](/wiki/Ash%27s_friends#Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series:_XY "Ash's friends"): the [Lumiose City](/wiki/Lumiose_City "Lumiose City") [Gym Leader](/wiki/Gym_Leader "Gym Leader") [Clemont](/wiki/Clemont_(anime) "Clemont (anime)"), his younger sister [Bonnie](/wiki/Bonnie_(anime) "Bonnie (anime)"), and a rookie [Performer](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Performer "Pokémon Performer") named [Serena](/wiki/Serena_(anime) "Serena (anime)").
