**Charmander** ([Japanese](/wiki/List_of_Japanese_Pok%C3%A9mon_names "List of Japanese Pokémon names"): **ヒトカゲ** *Hitokage*) is a [Fire-type](/wiki/Fire_(type) "Fire (type)") [Pokémon](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_(species) "Pokémon (species)") introduced in [Generation I](/wiki/Generation_I "Generation I").

It [evolves](/wiki/Evolution "Evolution") into [Charmeleon](/wiki/Charmeleon_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Charmeleon (Pokémon)") starting at [level](/wiki/Level "Level") 16, which evolves into [Charizard](/wiki/Charizard_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Charizard (Pokémon)") starting at level 36.

Along with [Bulbasaur](/wiki/Bulbasaur_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Bulbasaur (Pokémon)") and [Squirtle](/wiki/Squirtle_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Squirtle (Pokémon)"), Charmander is one of the three [first partner Pokémon of Kanto](/wiki/Kanto_first_partner_Pok%C3%A9mon "Kanto first partner Pokémon") available at the beginning of [Pokémon Red, Green](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Red_and_Green_Versions "Pokémon Red and Green Versions"), [Blue](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Red_and_Blue_Versions "Pokémon Red and Blue Versions"), [FireRed, and LeafGreen](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen_Versions "Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen Versions").

## Biology

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/3/3e/Ash_Charmander_waiting_for_Damian.png/250px-Ash_Charmander_waiting_for_Damian.png)](/wiki/File:Ash_Charmander_waiting_for_Damian.png)Charmander protecting the flame on its tail from the rain

Charmander is a bipedal, [reptilian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/reptile "wp:reptile") [Pokémon](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_(species) "Pokémon (species)") with a primarily orange body and blue eyes. Its underside from the chest down and the soles of its feet are cream-colored. It has two small fangs visible in its upper jaw and two smaller fangs in its lower jaw. A fire burns at the tip of this Pokémon's slender tail, which has blazed there since Charmander's birth.

The flame can indicate Charmander's health and mood, burning brightly when the Pokémon is strong, weakly when it is exhausted, wavering when it is happy, and blazing when it is enraged. It is said that Charmander would die if its flame were to go out. However, if the Pokémon is healthy, the flame will continue to burn even if it gets a bit wet and is said to steam in the rain. Charmander can be found in hot, [mountainous areas](/wiki/List_of_Pok%C3%A9mon_by_habitat#Mountain_Pok%C3%A9mon "List of Pokémon by habitat"). It has been recently seen living in the [Terarium](/wiki/Terarium "Terarium") of [Blueberry Academy](/wiki/Blueberry_Academy "Blueberry Academy"). However, it is found far more often in the ownership of [Trainers](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Trainer "Pokémon Trainer"). As seen in [Pokémon Snap](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Snap "Pokémon Snap") and [New Pokémon Snap](/wiki/New_Pok%C3%A9mon_Snap "New Pokémon Snap"), Charmander exhibits pack behavior, calling others of its species if it finds food and watching the flames on each other's tails to ensure they don't go out. As shown in [Pokémon Sleep](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Sleep "Pokémon Sleep"), Charmander is known to sleep while curled up. Supposedly, it draws warmth from the flame on its tail. During quiet nights, the sounds of its flame can be heard by listening carefully while Charmander sleeps.[[1]](#cite_note-1)

## Game data

### NPC appearances

- [PokéPark Wii: Pikachu's Adventure](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9Park_Wii:_Pikachu%27s_Adventure "PokéPark Wii: Pikachu's Adventure"): Charmander is one of [Pikachu](/wiki/Pikachu_(Pok%C3%A9Park) "Pikachu (PokéPark)")'s best friends. After falling through the mysterious tunnel, he almost lands into a pool of molten lava, only to be saved by the [Lava Zone](/wiki/Lava_Zone "Lava Zone") leader, [Blaziken](/wiki/Blaziken_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Blaziken (Pokémon)"), who takes him in as his pupil and trains to become stronger. After he's been found, he journeys to the [Meeting Place](/wiki/Meeting_Place "Meeting Place"), where he helps [Piplup](/wiki/Piplup_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Piplup (Pokémon)") mend the balloon and makes fireworks. He later accompanies Pikachu to the [Sky Pavilion](/wiki/Sky_Pavilion "Sky Pavilion") along with [Piplup](/wiki/Piplup_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Piplup (Pokémon)") and [Chikorita](/wiki/Chikorita_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Chikorita (Pokémon)"), where he meets [Mew](/wiki/Mew_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Mew (Pokémon)"). Once the [Sky Prism](/wiki/Sky_Prism "Sky Prism") has been restored, Charmander celebrates by firing off the fireworks he made.

## In animation

### Main series

[![](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/b/b1/Charmander_anime.png/250px-Charmander_anime.png)](/wiki/File:Charmander_anime.png)Charmander in [*Pokémon Horizons: The Series*](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Horizons:_The_Series "Pokémon Horizons: The Series")

#### Major appearances

##### [Ash's Charmander](/wiki/Ash%27s_Charmander "Ash's Charmander")

Charmander physically debuted in *[Charmander - The Stray Pokémon](/wiki/EP011 "EP011")*, where [Ash](/wiki/Ash_Ketchum "Ash Ketchum"), [Misty](/wiki/Misty_(anime) "Misty (anime)"), and [Brock](/wiki/Brock_(anime) "Brock (anime)") found him after he was abandoned by his [Trainer](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Trainer "Pokémon Trainer"), [Damian](/wiki/Damian "Damian"), who only cared about raising strong Pokémon. At first, Charmander refused to leave the spot he was at, believing Damian would return for him. However, when a rainstorm picked up, Charmander grew ill and Ash took him to a [Pokémon Center](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Center "Pokémon Center"). Charmander eventually realized that Damian had been abusing him, and allowed Ash to [catch](/wiki/Caught_Pok%C3%A9mon "Caught Pokémon") him. He later evolved into Charmeleon in *[The March of the Exeggutor Squad](/wiki/EP043 "EP043")*, and then into Charizard in *[Attack of the Prehistoric Pokémon](/wiki/EP046 "EP046")*.

Ash's Charmander also appeared in *[I Choose You!](/wiki/M20 "M20")*, which is set in a different continuity from [*Pokémon the Series*](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_the_Series "Pokémon the Series"). In this continuity, it previously belonged to [Cross](/wiki/Cross "Cross") and evolved into Charmeleon after defeating a [Jigglypuff](/wiki/Jigglypuff_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Jigglypuff (Pokémon)") in [battle](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_battle "Pokémon battle"). It later evolved into Charizard while battling Cross's [Incineroar](/wiki/Incineroar_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Incineroar (Pokémon)").

##### [Zippo](/wiki/Zippo "Zippo")

[Ritchie](/wiki/Ritchie "Ritchie") owned a Charmander [nicknamed](/wiki/Nickname "Nickname") [Zippo](/wiki/Zippo "Zippo"), which debuted in *[A Friend In Deed](/wiki/EP078 "EP078")*. It would evolve into Charmeleon by its appearance in *[A Parent Trapped!](/wiki/EP221 "EP221")*.

##### [Team Go-Getters](/wiki/Team_Go-Getters "Team Go-Getters")

A Charmander starred in *[Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Team Go-Getters Out of the Gate!](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon:_Team_Go-Getters_Out_of_the_Gate! "Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Team Go-Getters Out of the Gate!")* as the leader of Team Go-Getters.

##### [Alain's Charmander](/wiki/Alain%27s_Charmander "Alain's Charmander")

A Charmander appeared in a flashback in *[Mega Evolution Special I](/wiki/XYS01 "XYS01")* as the unevolved form of [Alain](/wiki/Alain "Alain")'s Charizard.

##### [Trevor's Charmander](/wiki/Trevor%27s_Charmander "Trevor's Charmander")

[Trevor](/wiki/Trevor_(anime) "Trevor (anime)")'s Charmander first appeared in *[Summer of Discovery!](/wiki/XY039 "XY039")*. It would be revealed to have evolved into Charmeleon by *[A Legendary Photo Op!](/wiki/XY086 "XY086")*, and then into Charizard by *[A League of His Own!](/wiki/XY125 "XY125")*.

##### [Leon's Charmander](/wiki/Leon%27s_Charmander "Leon's Charmander")

In *[Sword and Shield: The Darkest Day!](/wiki/JN043 "JN043")*, [Leon](/wiki/Leon_(anime) "Leon (anime)")'s Charizard was revealed to have been a Charmander in a flashback. It made more flashback appearances later in [*Pokémon Journeys: The Series*](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Journeys:_The_Series "Pokémon Journeys: The Series").

##### Other

In *[Journey to the Starting Line!](/wiki/HS14 "HS14")*, [Gilbert](/wiki/Gilbert_(HS14) "Gilbert (HS14)") intended to start his [journey](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_journey "Pokémon journey") as a [Pokémon Trainer](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Trainer "Pokémon Trainer"), but the trio of Kanto first partner Pokémon were scared off when [Ash's Tauros](/wiki/Ash%27s_Tauros "Ash's Tauros") stampeded through [Professor Oak](/wiki/Professor_Oak_(anime) "Professor Oak (anime)")'s house. Professor Oak, [Tracey](/wiki/Tracey_Sketchit "Tracey Sketchit"), [Delia](/wiki/Delia_Ketchum "Delia Ketchum"), and Gilbert split up to find the three missing Pokémon. The Charmander was the first one to be found.

In *[A Six Pack Attack!](/wiki/AG071 "AG071")*, a Charmander went with Professor Oak on his trip to [Hoenn](/wiki/Hoenn "Hoenn") along with a [Bulbasaur](/wiki/Bulbasaur_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Bulbasaur (Pokémon)") and [Squirtle](/wiki/Squirtle_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Squirtle (Pokémon)").

### The Electric Tale of Pikachu

*Main article: [Ash's Charmander](/wiki/Ash%27s_Charmander "Ash's Charmander")*

Ash is shown to own a Charmander despite this, his own Charmander first appears in *[You Gotta Have Friends](/wiki/ET08 "ET08")*. Ash's Charmander's capture is not shown in the manga, instead, when Charmander first appears from its [Poké Ball](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9_Ball_(item) "Poké Ball (item)"), it is simply stated that "Ash has been busy since the last comic".

Ash's Charmander reappears as a [Charizard](/wiki/Charizard_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Charizard (Pokémon)") in the chapter *[The Indigo Finals](/wiki/ET13 "ET13")* at the [Indigo Plateau Conference](/wiki/Indigo_Plateau_Conference "Indigo Plateau Conference"). In this chapter, Charizard battles [Charley](/wiki/Zippo "Zippo"), [Ritchie](/wiki/Ritchie "Ritchie")'s own Charizard.

### Concept and development

Charmander and its evolved forms were designed by [Atsuko Nishida](/wiki/Atsuko_Nishida "Atsuko Nishida").[[2]](#cite_note-2)[[3]](#cite_note-3)

### Design variations

In its [Generation I](/wiki/Generation_I "Generation I") [sprites](#Sprites) and original key artwork, Charmander is depicted with a small line of spikes on its back. These spikes are also seen on [Blue's Charmander](/wiki/Blue%27s_Charmander "Blue's Charmander") in the [Pokémon Adventures](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Adventures "Pokémon Adventures") manga, where the spikes are larger and easier to see, and on [Green's Charmander](/wiki/Green%27s_Charizard "Green's Charizard") in the [Pokémon Pocket Monsters](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Pocket_Monsters "Pokémon Pocket Monsters") manga.

Additionally, Charmander is depicted with [an inconsistent number of fingers](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_controversies#Four-fingered_hands "Pokémon controversies") in its designs. The original sprites designed by [Atsuko Nishida](/wiki/Atsuko_Nishida "Atsuko Nishida") for [Pokémon Red and Green](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Red_and_Green_Versions "Pokémon Red and Green Versions") featured three fingers, while the artwork [Ken Sugimori](/wiki/Ken_Sugimori "Ken Sugimori") drew based on those sprites had four fingers. Currently, the [animated series](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_animated_series "Pokémon animated series"), [movies](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_movie "Pokémon movie"), and [specials](/wiki/List_of_animated_series_specials "List of animated series specials"), as well as artwork unrelated to [games](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_games "Pokémon games") and [merchandise](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_merchandise "Pokémon merchandise"), use the three-finger design; while the key artwork used since [Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_FireRed_and_LeafGreen_Versions "Pokémon FireRed and LeafGreen Versions"), as well as the games' 3D models and artwork based on those (such as artwork for [Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon:_Let%27s_Go,_Pikachu!_and_Let%27s_Go,_Eevee! "Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! and Let's Go, Eevee!")), feature the four-finger design. The number of fingers used for [TCG](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Trading_Card_Game "Pokémon Trading Card Game") card art often varies between illustrators, and even within a single illustrator's works; compare [Boundaries Crossed](/wiki/Boundaries_Crossed_(TCG) "Boundaries Crossed (TCG)") [Charmander](/wiki/Charmander_(Boundaries_Crossed_18) "Charmander (Boundaries Crossed 18)") and [Generations](/wiki/Generations_(TCG) "Generations (TCG)") [Charmander](/wiki/Charmander_(Generations_RC3) "Charmander (Generations RC3)"), both illustrated by [Akira Komayama](/wiki/Akira_Komayama "Akira Komayama").

### Origin



[![Bulbanews](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/thumb/9/96/Bulbanews_logo.png/50px-Bulbanews_logo.png)](/wiki/File:Bulbanews_logo.png "Bulbanews")Bulbanews



[Bulbanews](https://bulbanews.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Front_page "n:Front page") has an article related to this subject:

***[On the Origin of Species: Charmander](https://bulbanews.bulbagarden.net/wiki/On_the_Origin_of_Species:_Charmander "n:On the Origin of Species: Charmander")***

Charmander's design was based on Charizard in such a way that it was surprising to see it evolving into the latter Pokémon.[[4]](#cite_note-Origin-4) In particular, Charmander was designed as a [lizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/lizard "wp:lizard").[[4]](#cite_note-Origin-4) Based on its name, Charmander is also based on a [salamander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/salamander "wp:salamander"), which are amphibians that physically resemble lizards. [In folklore and mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_depictions_of_salamanders "wp:Cultural depictions of salamanders"), salamanders are often thought of as fire spirits capable of surviving in extreme heat or flames. As Charizard is based on a [European dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_dragon "wp:European dragon"), Charmander's lizardlike design seems to reference how Western dragons in folklore are heavily inspired by lizards and other reptiles, as well as how various types of lizards are likened to (and named after) dragons due to this connection. Its bipedal design resembles a baby [theropod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/theropod "wp:theropod") dinosaur.

#### Name origin

Charmander is a combination of *char* (to burn) and *salamander*, as stated in the January 1999 "POCKEMON JOURNAL" on the official [Game Freak](/wiki/Game_Freak "Game Freak") website.[[5]](#cite_note-5) Alternatively, in a 2016 interview with [Tsunekazu Ishihara](/wiki/Tsunekazu_Ishihara "Tsunekazu Ishihara") by INSIDE, Charmander is stated to be a combination of *[charcoal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/charcoal "wp:charcoal")* (carbon residue produced by strongly heating wood) and *[Salamandra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salamandra "wp:Salamandra")* (a genus of salamander including four species of fire salamander).[[6]](#cite_note-6)

Hitokage may be a combination of 火 *hi* (fire) and 蜥蜴 *tokage* (lizard), forming together the word 火蜥蜴 *hitokage* (mythical salamander).
