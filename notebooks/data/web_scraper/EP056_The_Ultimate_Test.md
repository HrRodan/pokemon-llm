## Blurb

*Ash signs up to take an exam to be admitted to the Pokémon League. But judging by his awful score on the multiple-choice quiz, it may be a trivial pursuit. He enters the battling portion of the test hoping to raise his score. Also taking the test are Jessie and James, disguised as nerdy students. But the dastardly duo won't stand for doing anything the honest way, and they each get expelled from the test. Ash battles the instructor with a set of three Pokémon randomly assigned for the test. They turn out to be an Arbok, a Weezing, and a Meowth. What were the chances? Ash knows the Poison-type Pokémon's attacks pretty well from being on the receiving end of them so often. He fights a good match, but loses in the end. Just then, Team Rocket bursts in with the Pokémon James was given for the test. These powerful Pokémon will help Team Rocket nab all the Pokémon at the testing facility—or so it thinks. The Pokémon are obedient to the instructor who trained them, and they turn on Team Rocket. With a little help from Ash, Team Rocket is sent blasting off again. The testing instructor offers to let Ash take the test again—he's sure Ash can pass this time—but Ash would rather enter the League his own way, by traveling, collecting badges, and seeing all the world of Pokémon has to offer.*

## Plot

As [Ash](/wiki/Ash_Ketchum "Ash Ketchum") and [his friends](/wiki/Ash%27s_friends "Ash's friends"), including their new friend [Todd](/wiki/Todd_Snap "Todd Snap"), are having lunch, [Misty](/wiki/Misty_(anime) "Misty (anime)") points out that Ash has not won a [Gym](/wiki/Gym "Gym") [Badge](/wiki/Badge "Badge") in a while. Ash claims he wins all of his [battles](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_battle "Pokémon battle") with skill, and challenges Misty to one. Todd steps in and tells Ash that he should take the [Pokémon League Admissions Exam](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_League_Admissions_Exam "Pokémon League Admissions Exam").

The group arrives at the Pokémon League Admissions Center, and Ash signs up and is assigned testing number 7. Nearby, [Jessie](/wiki/Jessie "Jessie"), [disguised](/wiki/List_of_Team_Rocket%27s_disguises "List of Team Rocket's disguises") as a schoolgirl, complains that she didn't get number 7, causing Ash to briefly become suspicious. A [Nurse Joy](/wiki/Nurse_Joy "Nurse Joy") enters and an infatuated [Brock](/wiki/Brock_(anime) "Brock (anime)") follows her. Ash is told by the [head instructor](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_League_entrance_exam_instructor "Pokémon League entrance exam instructor") to leave all his [Poké Balls](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9_Ball_(item) "Poké Ball (item)") at the counter, as well as temporarily leave [Pikachu](/wiki/Ash%27s_Pikachu "Ash's Pikachu") in Misty's care. Nurse Joy, who turns out to be from [Fuchsia City](/wiki/Fuchsia_City "Fuchsia City"), tells Ash and his friends that she signed up for the exam because it was a convenient way for her to train to become a [Pokémon Master](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Master "Pokémon Master").

Soon after, [James](/wiki/James "James") walks in, disguised as a schoolboy with glasses, but quickly heads for the counter once Ash sees him. When the head instructor reads James's registration form and calls out his name, James tells him to keep quiet. Unfortunately, Jessie hears this, takes James outside, and asks what he is doing there. James confidently says that he too wants to become a Pokémon Master, then asks Jessie what she is doing here. Jessie explains that she wants to add another talent to her collection, just as [Meowth](/wiki/Meowth_(Team_Rocket) "Meowth (Team Rocket)") shows up and scratches both their faces. James quickly throws Meowth a ball of yarn as a distraction, and then runs off with Jessie back inside for the exam.

The first exam tests a person's knowledge of [Pokémon](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_(species) "Pokémon (species)"), where they answer true or false questions. Ash does his best to answer each of the questions correctly. At the [Magikarp](/wiki/Magikarp_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Magikarp (Pokémon)") question, James is reminded of [the](/wiki/EP015 "EP015") [time](/wiki/EP016 "EP016") he had a [Magikarp](/wiki/James%27s_Magikarp "James's Magikarp"), causing him to briefly shake in anger. He notices Jessie simply giving each question a "true" answer, hoping that she gets half of them right.

The second exam tests a person's ability to recognize Pokémon. The Pokémon to recognize on the screen looks like a ball; Ash thinks it is a [Voltorb](/wiki/Voltorb_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Voltorb (Pokémon)"), James thinks it is a Poké Ball, and Jessie thinks it is an [Electrode](/wiki/Electrode_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Electrode (Pokémon)"). The head instructor reveals that it is a [Jigglypuff](/wiki/Jigglypuff_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Jigglypuff (Pokémon)"), as seen from above, to everyone's disbelief. The next Pokémon to recognize looks like a swirl; Ash guesses it is a [Poliwhirl](/wiki/Poliwhirl_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Poliwhirl (Pokémon)"), James believes it is an [Omanyte](/wiki/Omanyte_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Omanyte (Pokémon)"), and Jessie guesses it is an [Arbok](/wiki/Arbok_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Arbok (Pokémon)") when it was all coiled up. However, the correct answer is a [Poliwag](/wiki/Poliwag_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Poliwag (Pokémon)"), as its swirl changes direction upon it [evolving](/wiki/Evolution "Evolution"). The next Pokémon for the students to guess has a fiery tail, and Ash guesses it is a [Charmander](/wiki/Charmander_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Charmander (Pokémon)"), but the right answer was [Ponyta](/wiki/Ponyta_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Ponyta (Pokémon)"). A furious Jessie demands that she be asked easier questions, and the head instructor expels her. Once outside, Jessie wakes up Meowth and says that they are going to get revenge.

When the scoreboard comes down, Nurse Joy is shown to be close to the top, while Ash, to his disbelief, realizes his score is one of the lowest. Ash is left frustrated when Todd takes some photos of his abysmal score. Meanwhile, James stands alone in a corner, vowing that he will try extra hard for Jessie.

The third exam involves Pokémon battles, as Nurse Joy uses a [Squirtle](/wiki/Squirtle_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Squirtle (Pokémon)") against a fellow instructor's Charmander. Next, the head instructor tells Ash and James that they each have to select a belt of three Poké Balls without knowing what Pokémon are inside them. They choose their belts, and James is up first against the head instructor. The first Pokémon James sends out is a [Pikachu](/wiki/Pikachu_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Pikachu (Pokémon)"), much to his delight. James believes that he cannot lose with Pikachu and claims that he has been destroyed by [Ash's Pikachu](/wiki/Ash%27s_Pikachu "Ash's Pikachu")'s attacks so many times that he knows them all by heart. The head instructor sends out a [Graveler](/wiki/Graveler_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Graveler (Pokémon)"). James immediately commands Pikachu to use [Thunderbolt](/wiki/Thunderbolt_(move) "Thunderbolt (move)"), but the attack doesn't work on Graveler, allowing it to send Pikachu flying with a [Tackle](/wiki/Tackle_(move) "Tackle (move)"). Angry at this, James sends out an [Ivysaur](/wiki/Ivysaur_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Ivysaur (Pokémon)") and a [Charizard](/wiki/Charizard_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Charizard (Pokémon)") at the same time. Unfortunately, the head instructor expels James for breaking the rules by bringing out two Pokémon at once.

It is Ash's turn to face the head instructor, who sends out a [Flareon](/wiki/Flareon_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Flareon (Pokémon)"). Ash pulls out his first Poké Ball, which reveals a [Weezing](/wiki/Weezing_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Weezing (Pokémon)"), much to Ash's shock and displeasure. The instructor has Flareon use [Flamethrower](/wiki/Flamethrower_(move) "Flamethrower (move)"), which Weezing proceeds to dodge. Coming up with an idea, Ash tells Weezing to counter with [Smog](/wiki/Smog_(move) "Smog (move)"), which explodes upon being hit by the Flamethrower, throwing Flareon backwards. Flareon uses [Leer](/wiki/Leer_(move) "Leer (move)"), but Ash has Weezing use [Smokescreen](/wiki/Smokescreen_(move) "Smokescreen (move)") to hide, then follow up with a Tackle, knocking Flareon out. As Misty comments on Ash being good at battling after all, Brock comments that even [Team Rocket](/wiki/Team_Rocket_trio "Team Rocket trio") could win if they battled like that. Meanwhile, Team Rocket is taking notes of Ash's fighting strategy. Next, the head instructor sends out a [Jolteon](/wiki/Jolteon_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Jolteon (Pokémon)"), and Ash sends out an Arbok. Jolteon uses [Agility](/wiki/Agility_(move) "Agility (move)"), but Arbok stops it with [Glare](/wiki/Glare_(move) "Glare (move)"). Arbok tries to use [Wrap](/wiki/Wrap_(move) "Wrap (move)"), but gets hurt by Jolteon's spikes. At this point, while Arbok is wincing in pain, Jolteon shakes off the Glare attack and finishes Arbok off with [Thunder](/wiki/Thunder_(move) "Thunder (move)").

For the final round, Ash opens his last Poké Ball to reveal a [Meowth](/wiki/Meowth_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Meowth (Pokémon)"), while the head instructor sends out a [Vaporeon](/wiki/Vaporeon_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Vaporeon (Pokémon)"). Meowth tries to use [Fury Swipes](/wiki/Fury_Swipes_(move) "Fury Swipes (move)"), but gets [frozen](/wiki/Freeze_(status_condition) "Freeze (status condition)") in its tracks when Vaporeon uses [Ice Beam](/wiki/Ice_Beam_(move) "Ice Beam (move)"), giving the victory to the head instructor. Team Rocket's Meowth scratches Ash's face and frees his fellow Meowth. The head instructor is impressed by the talking Meowth, so he goes to capture him. However, Team Rocket returns with James's Pikachu, Ivysaur, and Charizard. When the head instructor demands that they return those Pokémon immediately, Jessie and James say their [motto](/wiki/Team_Rocket_motto "Team Rocket motto") and remove their disguises. Team Rocket orders Ivysaur to use [Vine Whip](/wiki/Vine_Whip_(move) "Vine Whip (move)"), Charizard to use Flamethrower, and Pikachu to use Thunder. Before the Pokémon can attack, the head instructor quickly orders them to turn around, causing Pikachu, Ivysaur, and Charizard to attack Team Rocket. Ash realizes that those Pokémon listened to the head instructor because he [raised](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_training "Pokémon training") them. Ash orders his Meowth, Weezing, and Arbok to also stop Team Rocket. After Team Rocket has been beaten down by their own Pokémon, Weezing uses [Explosion](/wiki/Explosion_(move) "Explosion (move)"), sending them blasting off again.

Back inside the building, the head instructor tells the whole class that due to Team Rocket's interference, they have to do the entire exam all over again. The head instructor offers Ash another chance to pass the test, but Ash refuses, saying that he was better off winning Gym Badges from [Gym Leaders](/wiki/Gym_Leader "Gym Leader"). A short time later, Ash and his friends say goodbye to the head instructor and Nurse Joy before heading off for [Cinnabar Island](/wiki/Cinnabar_Island "Cinnabar Island"). Todd comes up to Nurse Joy with one of the two Meowth that got hurt in the Explosion. Unexpectedly, the Meowth talks, revealing that it is Team Rocket's Meowth, and leaps away to go after Jessie and James. Meanwhile, as Jessie and James vow revenge on Ash and his friends, they quickly discover that they ended up with the wrong Meowth.

## Trivia

- The Pokémon [Ash](/wiki/Ash_Ketchum "Ash Ketchum") uses in the exam are the same species as those that [Team Rocket](/wiki/Team_Rocket_trio "Team Rocket trio") uses to battle him on an everyday basis: [Arbok](/wiki/Arbok_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Arbok (Pokémon)"), [Weezing](/wiki/Weezing_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Weezing (Pokémon)"), and [Meowth](/wiki/Meowth_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Meowth (Pokémon)"). [James](/wiki/James "James") does almost the same thing in reverse: he gets a [Pikachu](/wiki/Pikachu_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Pikachu (Pokémon)"), [Ivysaur](/wiki/Ivysaur_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Ivysaur (Pokémon)"), and [Charizard](/wiki/Charizard_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Charizard (Pokémon)") to use; it would have been an exact correlation had [Ash's Bulbasaur](/wiki/Ash%27s_Bulbasaur "Ash's Bulbasaur") not refused to evolve [five episodes prior](/wiki/EP051 "EP051").
- This episode was parodied a few times in [the US](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_in_the_United_States "Pokémon in the United States") commercial for Pokémon that is aired on [Kids WB!](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_in_the_United_States "Pokémon in the United States").
- The theory test of this episode seems to be a reference to the questionnaire on the [games](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_games "Pokémon games")' [Cinnabar Gym](/wiki/Cinnabar_Gym "Cinnabar Gym"), which the player can use to bypass the Trainer battles up to [Blaine](/wiki/Blaine "Blaine"), the Gym Leader. In the [animated series](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_animated_series "Pokémon animated series"), Blaine just happens to be the Gym Leader who Ash is supposed to challenge next. However, unlike in the games, passing the tests results in instant qualification to the [Pokémon League](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_League "Pokémon League"), bypassing the requirement of eight Gym Badges to enter.
- Jessie and James's exam numbers (634 and 546, respectively) are hidden references to their Japanese names. Using a type of Japanese wordplay called [Goroawase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goroawase "wp:Goroawase"), the individual numbers can be read as "mu-sa-shi" (Musashi) and "ko-ji-ro" (Kojiro), respectively.
  * A similar wordplay about Jessie's Japanese name was later used in *[This Magik Moment!](/wiki/SM113 "SM113")*.


### Errors

- When Ash claims that he has skill and luck, his thumb is covered by his glove.
- When Ash hands over his [Poké Balls](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9_Ball_(item) "Poké Ball (item)") to the [Pokémon League entrance exam instructor](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_League_entrance_exam_instructor "Pokémon League entrance exam instructor"), there are five. When the instructor closes his hand, there are six.
- In one scene where the group talks to [Nurse Joy](/wiki/Nurse_Joy "Nurse Joy") and Todd takes pictures of Pokémon, there is a Trainer with a green-colored [Weedle](/wiki/Weedle_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Weedle (Pokémon)"). Coincidentally, Weedle is green when [Shiny](/wiki/Shiny_Pok%C3%A9mon "Shiny Pokémon") in [Gold and Silver](/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Gold_and_Silver_Versions "Pokémon Gold and Silver Versions"), albeit a different shade.

- When James hands over the exam form, he doesn't have a number pin. When he turns to look at [Jessie](/wiki/Jessie "Jessie") in the next shot, however, he is shown wearing a number pin.
- When Jessie says she was a costume designer, she has her left foot curled, but her reflection in the mirror shows it to be her right.
- When [Meowth](/wiki/Meowth_(Team_Rocket) "Meowth (Team Rocket)") is suspicious with Jessie, he has pads on his paws.
- When Meowth scratches both Jessie and James, there are four lines of light. However, Jessie and James's faces have only three marks.
- The computer's image of a [Caterpie](/wiki/Caterpie_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Caterpie (Pokémon)") leaves a section of its body colored purple on the screen.
- When Jessie says that the swirl is an Arbok, her eyes are green like James's instead of blue.
- When Ash, Jessie, and James are shocked when the instructor reveals the answer for the swirl question, Ash's right shoe is completely white.
- After Ash answers the tail flame question, and the view reverts to the big screen, the picture on the screen shows a flame from [Ponyta](/wiki/Ponyta_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Ponyta (Pokémon)")'s mane, not tail, but before Ash is answering, it shows the correct flame.

- The instructor says [Electric-type](/wiki/Electric_(type) "Electric (type)") moves do not work on [Rock](/wiki/Rock_(type) "Rock (type)") Pokémon, in reference to Pikachu's Thunderbolt failing to affect his [Graveler](/wiki/Graveler_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Graveler (Pokémon)"). - When Jessie says that there will not be a next time for the instructor and when their Pokémon turn against them, the inside of Ivysaur's ears is colored green instead of black.
- When Ash cheers for his victory over [Flareon](/wiki/Flareon_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Flareon (Pokémon)") with Weezing, the number seven on his jacket disappears in one frame.

### Dub edits

- [Kanto Pokérap](/wiki/Kanto_Pok%C3%A9rap "Kanto Pokérap"): Day 1 (Version 2)
- The excuses Jessie and James gave Meowth to go take the test were different:
  * Jessie in the Japanese version was going to attend a relative's wedding, while in the dub, it was a visit to a sick uncle.
  * James in the Japanese version was going to attend a relative's funeral, while in the dub, it was a dentist appointment.
- Similarly, Jessie's resume listing that acted as her motive for taking the test was also different:
  * In the Japanese version, she had tea-maker, flower arranger, kimono fitter, Sommelier, beautician, and shoe fitter.
  * In the dub, she had hostess, florist, costume designer, wine expert, beautician, and style consultant.
- Two of the slides were different in the original Japanese version. The question of [Vulpix](/wiki/Vulpix_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Vulpix (Pokémon)") evolving into [Ninetales](/wiki/Ninetales_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Ninetales (Pokémon)") via use of the [Fire Stone](/wiki/Fire_Stone "Fire Stone") was concocted by the 4Kids staff. The original question was whether the saying, "a Ninetales live for 1,000 years, a [Wartortle](/wiki/Wartortle_(Pok%C3%A9mon) "Wartortle (Pokémon)") live for 10,000 years" is correct or not. The Poliwag slide with Poliwrath on it originally had the correct Pokémon (Poliwhirl) next to Poliwag.
